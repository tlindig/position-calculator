How it works

  1. The tooltip is the item, the hovered button is the target, and the stage is the boundary. There is one calculator for each button.
  2. itemAt: "bottom right" and targetAt: "top right" put the bottom right corner of the tooltip on the top right corner of the button. The buttons sit at the right edge of the form, so the tooltip grows to the left and stays inside. targetOffset with y: -8 leaves a gap of 8 pixels.
  3. flip: "both" lets the library try the opposite placement when the first one overflows the boundary. mirror: true turns the gap around with it, so it is still 8 pixels, now below the button.
  4. The tooltip is hidden with hidden and has no size then. That is why the example calls resize() after showing it, and only then calculate().
  5. calculate() returns how far to move the tooltip from where it is now. The example adds moveBy to the current left and top.

Variations

  • In the playground, change flip to "none" and hover the i next to Username: the tooltip stays above and leaves the box. distance.overflow in the result says which edge.
  • Add stick: "all" to pull the tooltip back inside the box when no placement fits completely.
  • Use targetAt: "middle right" and itemAt: "middle left" for a tooltip at the side.
